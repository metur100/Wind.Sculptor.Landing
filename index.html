About the game

The relaxing puzzle game where the wind does the work

Wind Sculptor is a free, calm and satisfying puzzle game for Android and iPhone. You never touch a particle. Instead you swipe across the screen to create gusts of wind and blow drifting sand, autumn leaves, snowflakes, confetti, fireflies and bubbles into a glowing outline before the timer runs out. Faster swipes blow harder, gentle swipes nudge. Loose particles become wind trails, and wind trails become a finished sculpture.

30 handcrafted levels take you through six dreamy worlds: Sand Dunes, Autumn Park, Winter Peaks, Festival, Night Forest and Deep Ocean. Each material has its own physics. Heavy sand settles quickly, leaves flutter, snow floats, confetti spins, fireflies wander and bubbles always rise. New challenges keep it fresh: crosswinds, fans, walls, icy ponds, drains, limited wind energy, moving targets and two sculptures at once.

It's a game to breathe out with. Relaxing generative music, gentle sound effects and haptic feedback make every sculpture feel good. Collect a card for every shape, keep your streak in the Daily Challenge and play anywhere: no ads, no in-app purchases, no account, no tracking and no internet needed. Suitable for kids and adults.

Support

Need a gentle push?

Answers to the most common questions. Still stuck? Just write.

I can't fill the shape in time. Any tips?

Use short, slow swipes close to the particles for control and long, fast swipes to move a whole cloud. If the timer runs out you can keep sculpting, retry, or use a free hint.

How do I get three stars?

Fill more of the outline. The match bar at the top shows how close you are, and the star markers on it show each threshold.

The particles move on their own. Is that a bug?

No – that's the material. Fireflies wander, bubbles rise, snow floats and fans or crosswinds keep blowing. Plan your gusts around them.

I lost my progress. Can you restore it?

Progress is stored only on your device. We never receive it, so it can't be restored after uninstalling the game or resetting progress.

Does the game need an internet connection?

No. Everything, including the Daily Challenge, works fully offline.

Are there ads or in-app purchases?

No. The game is completely free, and hints are free too.

How do I delete my data?

Use Settings → Reset progress in the game, or uninstall it. Nothing is stored anywhere else. See the privacy policy.
